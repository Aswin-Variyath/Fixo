Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to become a Tasker? expand_more
You need to be at least 18 years old, have a valid ID, a local bank account, and the skills/tools required for the services you plan to offer.
How do I start receiving jobs? expand_more
Once your profile is completed and identity verified, you can set your work area and categories. Jobs matching your profile will appear in your dashboard.
Where is Fixo currently available? expand_more
Fixo is expanding rapidly across major metropolitan areas. Check our city directory in the app to see if we're active in your location.
How long does verification take? expand_more
Most identity and background checks are completed within 3–5 business days, though some may take longer depending on local processing times.
Do I need professional experience? expand_more
While professional certification is required for certain trades like electrical or plumbing, many tasks like cleaning or general help only require proven competence and reliability.
How and when do I get paid? expand_more
Payments are processed securely through the platform. Funds are typically available for withdrawal within 48–72 hours after a task is completed and confirmed.
What types of services can I offer? expand_more
Fixo covers a wide range of categories including handyman services, cleaning, electrical, plumbing, moving help, landscaping, and more.
